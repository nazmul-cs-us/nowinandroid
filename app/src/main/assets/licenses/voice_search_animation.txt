Voice-search animation attribution

The listening and transcribing motion is a vector adaptation of the Apple Face ID
Lottie Animation community resource by Mau Ali. The app uses only vector Lottie
shapes derived from the supplied SVG sequence; the exported PNG frames are not
packaged in the application.
