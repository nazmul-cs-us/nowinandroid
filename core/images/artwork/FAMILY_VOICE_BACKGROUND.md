# Family voice-call backdrop

`app/src/main/res/drawable-nodpi/family_voice_pattern.webp` is a cropped, resized
decorative background, not a contact photograph.

- Source: The Metropolitan Museum of Art, Islamic Art, object **447013**, Tile Panel.
- Record: https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/447013
- Image: https://images.metmuseum.org/CRDImages/is/original/DT11978.jpg
- Rights: public domain, The Met Open Access (CC0).
- Visually reviewed 2026-11-10: floral and geometric tilework, no people or animals.
- Processing: central 68% crop, 1000px-wide WebP.

Screenshots and preview captures belong in temporary storage, not this directory.
