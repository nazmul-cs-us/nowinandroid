DEENLY FAMILY

Stay within reach.

Receive a call alert when Deenly is closed. Open the alert, then open Deenly to answer while the caller is still ringing.

On iPhone

Requires iOS 16.4 or later. In Safari, choose Share → Add to Home Screen. Open Call Alerts from its new Home Screen icon before enabling notifications.

Connect your phone

In Deenly, open Family → Temporary call alerts. Copy its pairing code and paste it here. Codes expire after five minutes.

Temporary alerts expire after 24 hours. Sign out of Deenly while connected, or disable alerts here, to revoke this installation’s alerts. Delivery depends on the browser, notification permission and network; this helper does not automatically answer calls or start a native iOS call screen.
